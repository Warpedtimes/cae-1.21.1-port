## The original pack

**Create: Arcane Engineering** was made by **FortnitePro420Xx**. The quests, the scripts, the progression, the structures and the look of the pack are theirs. This port only moves that work to a newer Minecraft. It is shared with the author's permission.

The original pack: <https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/modpacks/create-arcane-engineering>

## The port

The 1.21.1 port was done by **Warpedtimes**, with the help of Claude (an AI assistant by Anthropic).

## Every mod in the pack

Every mod below belongs to its authors. The pack only downloads and uses them. The "Author(s)" column comes from the CurseForge or Modrinth project page on the date of this release (19 Oct 2026). If a name is missing or wrong, please open an issue and it will be fixed. Mods with the label "(team)" are published by a team or organisation; the people after it are the members listed on the project.

The pack also contains one mod of its own, `cae-3.0.0.jar` (source in `core/`). It is part of the port.
